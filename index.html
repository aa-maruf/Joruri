ABOUT JORURI

Making Public-Service Information Easier to Find

Joruri (জরুরি) is a public-service contact directory designed to help people in Bangladesh quickly find and access important public-service phone numbers.

Why Joruri?

Bangladesh has many important public and government services, but people may not always know which number to call when they need assistance.

While almost everyone is familiar with the national emergency number 999, fewer people know about specialized public-service hotlines such as child helplines, healthcare support, or legal assistance. When citizens are unaware of these dedicated services, they often rely solely on 999 even when another specialized hotline would be more direct and appropriate.

Joruri exists to bridge this gap by making specialized public services easy to discover, understand, and reach.

Our Purpose

"Joruri aims to make public-service information easier to discover and understand, helping people identify the service they need and reach it more easily."
